# Credits

This Repo contains no original Model. It is a Benchmark and a Set of Fine-Tunes
built on other People's Work.

## The Model under Test

**Laya**, `convaiinnovations/laya`, Apache 2.0, by
[Convai Innovations](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations), Author Nandakishor M.
Source at [github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya).
421M Parameters: `answerdotai/ModernBERT-large` plus a Decision Head trained with
RLCD. All Fine-Tunes here start from that Checkpoint, and the Inference Runtime in
`bench/` is that Project's Code with small Changes.

The Backbone is **ModernBERT-large** by Answer.AI and LightOn, Apache 2.0.

## The Model it is compared against

**Jev** by [TypeSafe AI](https://typesafe.ai), released 15 September 2026, closed
Weights. Every Jev Number in this Repo is quoted from a published Source, not
measured by me. I have no Access to it. Named after the Economist William Stanley
Jevons.

**Claude Haiku 4.5** Numbers are quoted from jev-phishing-bench, also not measured
here.

## Benchmarks

- **PhishNChips v5.2**, `AreLit/PhishNChips` on the Hub, Source at
  [github.com/R-Lit/PhishNChips](https://github.com/R-Lit/PhishNChips), MIT.
  2000 Emails built around real Phishing URLs.
- **jev-phishing-bench** by
  [anisselbd](https://github.com/anisselbd/jev-phishing-bench). The Jev and Haiku
  Reference Numbers on the Phishing Task come from there.
- **LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions**, Apache 2.0. A community Rebuild of Jev's four
  Benchmark Workflows, with soft Probability Targets. The Jev 1.13.0 Row and the
  0.735 Teacher Ceiling come from its Documentation.

## Research the Method comes from

- Xu et al. 2018, *A Semantic Loss Function for Deep Learning with Symbolic
  Knowledge*. The Weighted-Model-Counting Form of the Consistency Penalty.
- Asai and Hajishirzi 2020, *Logic-Guided Data Augmentation and Regularization*.
  The Claim that inverted-Pair Augmentation alone is not enough, which did not
  replicate here.
- Kumar et al. 2022, *Fine-Tuning can Distort Pretrained Features and Underperform
  Out-of-Distribution*. LP-FT.
- Wortsman et al. 2022, *Robust fine-tuning of zero-shot models*. WiSE-FT, tested
  here and it did not help.
- Charoenphakdee et al. 2021, on Focal Loss and proper Scoring Rules. The Reason
  Focal Loss was rejected.
- Guo et al. 2017, *On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks*. Temperature
  Scaling and ECE.
- Platt 1999, for the Scaling used in `bench/platt.py`.

## Backbone tried and rejected

- `EuroBERT/EuroBERT-610m`, Apache 2.0. Loads and trains, but needs far more
  Compute than this Experiment gave it.
- `chandar-lab/NeoBERT`, MIT. Not benchmarked. It requires `xformers`, and every
  published Wheel is built for Python 3.9/3.10 against Torch 2.10+cu128. On Torch
  2.14+cu132 with Python 3.13 the Kernels are inert and the Model silently returns
  NaN rather than failing loudly.
