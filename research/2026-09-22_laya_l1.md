## Question

Does the Laya decision head (type embedding + option-marker scorer + masked
per-question softmax) transfer unchanged onto a 33M-class encoder
(MiniLM-L12-H384) and clear the MiniLM-L6 specialist baseline (0.587) on the
official LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions test split with soft-CE training only?

## Hypothesis

Yes: the head is encoder-agnostic (the original stacks it on ModernBERT-large;
the architecture carries no ModernBERT-specific component), and the published
notebook recipe (4 epochs, eff batch 64, lr 2.5e-5/1e-4, cosine, fp16) fits a
1,200-case dataset in minutes. Falsified if test accuracy < 0.587 (G2) or
training cannot complete within 4 GB allocated VRAM (G1).

## Rig

- Data: LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions config "all" (1,200 train / 400 test
  cases; ~6,000 / ~2,000 decisions). One packed sequence per (case, question):
  [CLS] <type> instructions [SEP] [MASK] opt... [SEP] state [SEP];
  options <= 48 tok, head segment <= 192, state fills 512 (right-truncated).
  Gold = per-option probability distributions (soft targets). Unalignable
  gold rows are skipped and counted, never guessed (build_stats.json).
- Model: MiniLM-L12-H384 (33M enc) + Laya head (~8M: type emb, 2x
  TransformerEncoderLayer d384 norm_first dropout 0.1, LayerNorm-Linear-GELU-
  Linear scorer, untrained act head) = ~41M total, fp32 master weights,
  fp16 autocast compute.
- Arm: single arm (no ablation inside the rung; recipe changes = new rung).
- Budget: 4 epochs, micro 8 x accum 8 (eff 64), AdamW wd 0.01, lr enc 2.5e-5 /
  head 1e-4, cosine -> 1e-6, clip 1.0, seed 42, ~376 updates, target wall < 1 h
  on Quadro RTX 4000 8 GB (machine flipped from TU09FBO mid-plan - recorded in
  ENVIRONMENT.md).
- Eval: official test split at every epoch end (curve reporting only; gates
  judged on the FINAL checkpoint) + eval_l1.py full metrics.

## Scale-transfer caveats

- This licenses "the head transfers to 33M-class encoders at 1,200-case
  fine-tune scale" ONLY. It says nothing about RLCD (E-63), the generalist
  mixture, or the 0.753 13-family benchmark (never measured here).
- The [MASK]-as-marker port is NOT byte-equivalent to Laya's <opt> token;
  if G2 fails, marker-token identity is suspect #1 (retry with an added
  <opt> token as a new rung, not a retune).
