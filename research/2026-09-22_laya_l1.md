# E-62 - Laya-line L1 smoke: MiniLM + Laya head, soft-CE (TASKS row 102)

Copy of research/_template_experiment.md skeleton. Pre-registration landed
BEFORE launch (repo rule; EXPERIMENTS.md E-62). L1+L2 user-approved
2026-09-22; L3 deferred on L1/L2 results.

## Question

Does the Laya decision head (type embedding + option-marker scorer + masked
per-question softmax) transfer unchanged onto a 33M-class encoder
(MiniLM-L12-H384) and clear the MiniLM-L6 specialist baseline (0.587) on the
official LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions test split with soft-CE training only?

## Hypothesis

Yes: the head is encoder-agnostic (the original stacks it on ModernBERT-large;
the architecture carries no ModernBERT-specific component), and the published
notebook recipe (4 epochs, eff batch 64, lr 2.5e-5/1e-4, cosine, fp16) fits a
1,200-case dataset in minutes. Falsified if test accuracy < 0.587 (G2) or
training cannot complete within 4 GB allocated VRAM (G1).

## Rig

- Data: LocalLLaMA/typed-decisions config "all" (1,200 train / 400 test
  cases; ~6,000 / ~2,000 decisions). One packed sequence per (case, question):
  [CLS] <type> instructions [SEP] [MASK] opt... [SEP] state [SEP];
  options <= 48 tok, head segment <= 192, state fills 512 (right-truncated).
  Gold = per-option probability distributions (soft targets). Unalignable
  gold rows are skipped and counted, never guessed (build_stats.json).
- Model: MiniLM-L12-H384 (33M enc) + Laya head (~8M: type emb, 2x
  TransformerEncoderLayer d384 norm_first dropout 0.1, LayerNorm-Linear-GELU-
  Linear scorer, untrained act head) = ~41M total, fp32 master weights,
  fp16 autocast compute.
- Arm: single arm (no ablation inside the rung; recipe changes = new rung).
- Budget: 4 epochs, micro 8 x accum 8 (eff 64), AdamW wd 0.01, lr enc 2.5e-5 /
  head 1e-4, cosine -> 1e-6, clip 1.0, seed 42, ~376 updates, target wall < 1 h
  on Quadro RTX 4000 8 GB (machine flipped from TU09FBO mid-plan - recorded in
  ENVIRONMENT.md).
- Eval: official test split at every epoch end (curve reporting only; gates
  judged on the FINAL checkpoint) + eval_l1.py full metrics.

## Gates and verdicts

Pre-registered (EXPERIMENTS.md E-62), judged on the FINAL checkpoint:

- G1 feasibility: peak allocated VRAM <= 4096 MiB, 4 epochs complete, no
  shared-memory spill. ACTUAL: 1660 MiB, 4/4 epochs, 286 s -> PASS.
- G2 capability: test accuracy >= 0.587. ACTUAL: 0.6205 -> PASS.
- G3 diagnostic (not a gate): post-hoc per-(type, option-count) temperature
  fit on a train calibration slice (train[::15][:400]) must not increase test
  ECE. ACTUAL: raw ECE 0.0673 -> temp ECE 0.0948 -> FAIL (diagnostic).

Verdict (honest): PASS on the pre-registered capability gates (G1+G2).
eval_report.json's top-level "verdict" field reads FAIL only because the
script conservatively requires all three entries to pass; G3 was registered
as a diagnostic. Recorded exactly that way in EXPERIMENTS.md. The raw model
is ALREADY well-calibrated (ECE 0.0673 vs Laya's reported 0.030-0.081 and
Luni's 0.156); the per-group temperature fit on a 400-item slice overfits
and hurt. Carry-forward: skip per-group temperature at this calib size, or
fit ONE global T on >= 2000 items.

## Levers to carry

- The Laya decision head transfers UNCHANGED to a 33M-class encoder: 0.6205
  vs MiniLM-L6's 0.587 (+3.4 pts) at ~1/4 the params of the 149M baseline
  (0.646). Head architecture is encoder-agnostic in practice.
- Notebook-parity recipe works as-is at 37M: 4 epochs / eff 64 / lr
  2.5e-5+1e-4 / cosine / fp16 -> converged (loss 1.17 -> 0.97), 286 s on
  Quadro RTX 4000. No instability, no grad-scaler events.
- Calibration comes nearly free from soft-CE + gold distributions: raw ECE
  0.0673 without any post-hoc step. Do NOT fit per-group temperatures on
  small (<1000 item) calibration slices.
- Per-qtype: noul 0.695 > choice 0.597 > score 0.583; per-workflow:
  agent_trace_observability hardest (0.528) - same shape as Laya's own
  profile. score questions are the weak primitive at this scale.
- [MASK]-as-option-marker works: permutation/rename structure is sound by
  construction (one marker per option, scored at its own position).

## Mistakes and gotchas

- Windows Python emits SyntaxWarning: invalid escape sequence for
  non-raw docstrings containing .\.venv paths - make module docstrings raw
  (r"""). Cosmetic but pollutes every run's stderr.
- Dataset-id gotchas (now in MEMORY.md): allenai/scitail config is
  tsv_format (not tsv); yelp_review_full has NO fancyzhx/ namespace.
- G3's per-group temperature overfit is a lesson, not a bug: 8 groups x
  400 calib items = ~50 items/group -> grid-fit temperatures memorize noise.
- pwsh Tee-Object console log landed at repo root; moved to
  runs/laya/l1/console.log at close-out.

## Scale-transfer caveats

- This licenses "the head transfers to 33M-class encoders at 1,200-case
  fine-tune scale" ONLY. It says nothing about RLCD (E-63), the generalist
  mixture, or the 0.753 13-family benchmark (never measured here).
- The [MASK]-as-marker port is NOT byte-equivalent to Laya's <opt> token;
  G2 passing suggests marker identity is not load-bearing at this scale, but
  an <opt>-token rung would be needed to prove it.
- Accuracy sits BETWEEN MiniLM-L6 (0.587) and ModernBERT-base (0.646) -
  consistent with the card's scaling table, not with any 421M claim.
  Zero-shot Laya behavior (near-chance) is irrelevant here: L1 is fine-tuned.
