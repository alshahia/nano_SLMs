## Question being answered
Can a ~50M-class model, from scratch, with the Laya strategy (domain pretrain → mixture
→ typed ladder) reach Laya/Jev-class decision scores? (Follows E-62/E-63/E-64.)

## Architecture & pretrain
BERT 6L/H512/FFN2048/8h, vocab 30522, seq 256 — **measures 35.1M encoder (+6.7M head =
41.77M)**, honestly below the nominal "~50M" (recorded, not changed post-registration).
MLM 15% masking, AdamW 6e-4 cosine, fp16: heldout loss 2.97 → 2.28 (ppl 19.5 → 9.81),
31,821 steps, 79 min wall (PASS ≤12h), peak 4,610 MiB (FAIL vs 4,096 — recorded).
Scheduled probe monitoring: 34 bench_log records; probe numbers are trends only.

## Ladder results (runs/laya/l3/train_summary.json)

  stage-A exit — no net forgetting**); phishing AUROC 0.589 → **0.6490**; perm 0.185.

## Verdict (honest)
1/4 gates. **The transfer hypothesis of E-64 is CONFIRMED and CLOSED**: domain-matched
pretraining moved zero-shot phishing AUROC 0.576 → 0.649 (within 0.03-0.04 of Laya/Jev)
— first phishing-gate pass in the project. **The capability hypothesis is REFUTED at this
scale**: 260M domain tokens ≪ ~1B general tokens for decision generalization; and the
from-scratch encoder's mixture ceiling (0.6625) was below the bar before stage B began.
Replay (15%) fully prevented stage-B forgetting (−0.0003), unlike L2 (−0.062) — the G1
failure mode moved from "forgetting" to "low ceiling". G3 augmentation (order-shuffle +
rename) did NOT produce permutation invariance (0.185, worse than unaugmented) — open
architecture lever, not a data lever.

## Levers if L3 line continues
1. Scale pretrain tokens 3-5× (compute ~4-7 h more) or distill from MiniLM teacher.
2. Perm invariance: train WITH block-surgery-permuted duplicates of the same item
   (invariance by construction) instead of repack shuffling.
3. Longer stage B / lower typed LR with higher replay — G2 rose monotonically (0.485 →
   0.5185) at epoch 4; not converged.
